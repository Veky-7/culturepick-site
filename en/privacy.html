CulturePick

Privacy Policy

CulturePick (the “app”) only handles what it needs to help you find classes, and only for as long as it needs it.

This is a translation for convenience. If anything differs, the Korean version applies.

1. Using the app without signing in

  • We do not collect personal information such as your name or contact details.
  • Saved items, sign-up alerts and parents’ neighborhoods are stored only on this phone. They are deleted when you delete the app.
  • The birth year you add under “My child” is used only on this phone to pick classes for that age, and is never sent to your account or our server. It is cleared when you log out or delete your account.
  • Your location is used only on this phone to find nearby classes, and is never sent anywhere.
  • When you type a place name (e.g. Gwanghwamun) in search, we send only the search words through the CulturePick server to Kakao Map search to find that place. Your location is not sent, and the CulturePick server does not record search words.
  • If the app has an error, we send only the error, app version, Android version and device model to the CulturePick server so we can fix it. Your name, location and saved items are not sent.
  • Because of the ads at the bottom of the app, the ad company (Google) may use your phone’s advertising ID, IP address and similar data (see section 5).

2. What we receive when you sign in with Kakao, NAVER or Apple

  • The member ID and nickname provided by Kakao or NAVER
  • If you sign in with Apple (iPhone), the member ID provided by Apple and the name it shares the first time (left blank if not shared)
  • Your saved items, sign-up alerts and parents’ neighborhoods in the app
  • We do not store Kakao, NAVER or Apple sign-in keys (tokens) on the CulturePick server.

We do not receive your email, phone number, birthday or gender. Children under 14 cannot sign up (we check before sign-up).

3. Why we use it

  • So you can keep your saved items and alerts when you change phones or reinstall the app.
  • We do not use it for anything else (such as ad messages or sales).

4. How long we keep it

  • Until you delete your account. Tap Me › Delete account to delete it right away.
  • If you disconnect CulturePick on the Kakao side (Kakao Account › Connected services) or leave Kakao, we delete your data as soon as Kakao notifies us.
  • If you signed up with NAVER, a NAVER confirmation screen opens once when you delete your account, and the NAVER connection is removed at the same time.

5. Do we share it?

We do not sell or hand it over to anyone. However, we use these companies’ services to run the app.

  • Google Firebase — the server that stores sign-in information and saved items, alerts and parents’ neighborhoods (Google Cloud · Seoul)
  • Google Cloud Storage — where the app downloads fresh class data every day (Seoul). No personal information is sent; only the IP address needed for the internet connection is left with Google.
  • Kakao Map search (Kakao Corp.) — place name search. Only the search words are sent; your name, location and saved items are not.
  • Google AdMob — the ads at the bottom of the app. Google may use your advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad viewing history to choose ads and prevent fraud. You can delete the advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in phone Settings › Google › Ads (or Privacy › Ads).

Transfers abroad

The Google services above are operated by Google LLC (USA). Sign-in data and saved items are stored in Seoul, but the following may be transferred abroad (the USA and other countries with Google facilities) while running the service and for ads (AdMob).

  • Recipient: Google LLC · Google Privacy Policy policies.google.com/privacy
  • What: advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad viewing history (ads) · member ID, nickname, saved items (server operation)
  • When and how: over the internet while you use the app
  • Why: showing ads and preventing fraudulent clicks, running the server
  • How long: ad data under Google’s policies, member data until you delete your account
  • If you do not want this: use the app without signing in or delete your account and member data will not be transferred. For ad data, delete the advertising ID or turn off personalized ads as above (ads will still appear).

6. Alerts

Sign-up alerts ring on this phone. You can turn them off anytime in phone settings.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. You can also delete it right away with Delete account in the app.
