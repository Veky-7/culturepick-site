What CulturePick is

An information app that brings scattered public and private class listings into one place. CulturePick does not handle sign-ups or payments; it links you to each organization’s website.

ServiceSearch and map guide for classes and cultural events (free)
InformationClass listings organizations publish for everyone (name, audience, days, time, fee, sign-up period, seats left), exhibitions, and Korea Tourism Organization festival data
Sign-up & paymentNot handled by us. Tapping [Sign up] opens the organization’s website
AccountNot required. Sign in only if you want to keep saved items and alerts across phones
RevenueA banner ad at the bottom of the app (Google AdMob). No paid features or gambling
Made for
  • Workers looking for yoga or swimming near home after work
  • Parents looking for weekend programs to enjoy with kids
  • Adult children finding senior classes near their parents’ home
  • Anyone looking for a festival this weekend

Screens

The app has four menus at the bottom: Map · Events · Saved · Me. These are real app screens (shown in Korean).

Map

Find on the map

Places near you appear as bubbles. The number is how many classes are open for sign-up now. Filter by open now, culture centers, museums, libraries and festivals.

Map › Place

Classes by place

Tap a bubble to see its classes: open now, opening soon, then ongoing. Call, directions and website buttons included.

Map › Class

Class details

Audience, days, time, fee, sign-up period, seats left and the source. [Sign up] takes you to the organization’s page.

Map › Filters

Filter by situation

This weekend, with kids, after work or seniors — see only the classes that fit.

Events

Festivals across Korea

See ongoing, this-weekend and free festivals by date or distance. Finished festivals drop off automatically.

Saved

Saved & sign-up alerts

Hearted classes and festivals gather here. Turn on an alert to be reminded at 8 pm the day before and about 30 minutes before sign-up opens. Saved on your phone without an account.

Principles

  • We collect only information organizations publish for everyone, and show the source and original link for every class.
  • We visit websites at human speed, a limited number of times a day, and skip sites that refuse collection.
  • Seats and dates can change after collection, so we ask you to double-check on the organization’s page.
  • Festival data comes from Korea Tourism Organization (TourAPI), with photo credits.

The full source list is on the sources page (Korean). To report an error or ask us to remove information, email us below.
